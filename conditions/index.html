Before your dive.

Practical booking information and links to Devocean’s published terms.

Confirm your booking terms in writing

The online terms and May 2026 PDF show different deposits and cancellation periods. This page does not replace a booking agreement. Ask the team which conditions apply before paying.

Published online terms

The website states a minimum 50% non-refundable deposit and a 48-hour cancellation threshold. Cenote and cavern trips require Open Water certification and a minimum age of 15. Site entrance fees, camera permits and tips are additional.

Read the original online terms

Downloadable price-list terms

The May 2026 PDF instead describes a 30–100% deposit and a seven-day cancellation threshold. It also covers payment charges, medical documentation, weather changes, training requirements and equipment.

Read the original price list

Plan the practical details

  • Confirm meeting points and return arrangements with the team.
  • Bring your certification and complete the requested paperwork.
  • Confirm equipment, fees, payment options and camera rules.
  • Tell the team promptly about any requested changes.
  • Wildlife sightings and specific conditions cannot be guaranteed.

Help protect the cenotes

Do not touch formations, disturb sediment, collect objects or interfere with wildlife. Follow the guide’s instructions and site-specific rules, including restrictions on sunscreen and repellents.
